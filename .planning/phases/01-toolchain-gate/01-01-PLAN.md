Freeze the current toolchain, pin every baseline command, and stand up the two safety nets that make the rest of the phase measurable: a pinned `svelte-check` invocation and an output-window pixel-diff gate with a committed pre-upgrade baseline.

Purpose: TOOL-03's "no new errors versus the pre-upgrade baseline" is unenforceable today — `svelte-check` resolves no tsconfig on `main` (there is no root `tsconfig.json`/`jsconfig.json`, verified absent) and `test:svelte` is unpinned under `continue-on-error: true` in CI.
